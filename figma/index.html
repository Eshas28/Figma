Room Types


Single occupancy Room

A single occupancy room is designed to comfortably accommodate one person, offering privacy and a peaceful environment. It typically features a single bed, a work desk, and essential amenities such as a wardrobe, television, and an attached bathroom.

Family Room

A family room is a spacious accommodation designed to comfortably host families or small groups. It usually includes multiple beds or a combination of beds and sofa beds, along with essential amenities like a television, wardrobe, seating area, and an attached bathroom.

Duplex room

A duplex room is a luxurious and stylish accommodation spread over two floors, connected by an internal staircase. It offers a unique blend of space and comfort, often featuring a living area on the lower level and a bedroom on the upper level. Duplex rooms are ideal for families, couples, or guests seeking extra privacy and elegance.
